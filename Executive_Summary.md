# Executive Summary: Consumer AI Performance & Optimization Platform
---

**Consumer AI Analytics & Insights Analyst:** Daniel Rodriguez III

**Date:** August 28, 2026

---

## Business Context
Carnival Cruise Line's Consumer AI Guest Assistant ("SeaSmart AI") was deployed to streamline guest service across mobile (Carnival Hub App), web portals, and in-cabin digital channels. This project establishes an automated analytics platform to track containment rates, intent recognition accuracy, user acceptance testing (UAT) prompt variations, and cost savings.

## Key Insights
1. **Containment & Cost Savings Realization:** Across 10,000 evaluated guest sessions, SeaSmart AI achieved an overall containment rate of **40.4%**, generating **$33,888.72 in net labor cost savings** by reducing contact center escalations.
2. **Technical Bottleneck:** *WiFi_Troubleshooting* exhibited high turn counts and escalation rates, signaling an urgent need for re-engineered decision trees and fallback handling.
3. **A/B Prompt Optimization:** Statistical hypothesis testing confirmed that **Variant v2.0 (PromptRefined)** achieved a statistically significant lift in task completion accuracy (**84.0% vs. 76.0%**, $p < 0.001$), proving the efficacy of continuous prompt optimization.

## Strategic Recommendations
* **Production Deployment:** Promote Variant v2.0 prompt architecture to production across all channels.
* **Targeted Retraining:** Re-evaluate dialog workflows for *WiFi_Troubleshooting* to lower fallback rates.
* **Continuous Monitoring:** Implement automated drift and sentiment alerts inside quarterly operational reviews.
